About Em_R_Mangoes

Growing Healthy — Eating Healthier

We know that food grown on healthy land with healthy practices is healthy food. We aim to help protect the nearby Indian River Lagoon by limiting fertilizer and pesticide use and favoring organic practices whenever possible — we're not fully organic, but we lean on organic methods and healthy soil wherever we can.

Our Story

Our family moved to this property in Grant/Valkaria, Florida in 2017. Em_R_Mangoes wasn't even a thought back then — it started after clearing brush and realizing we had the room, and the itch, to grow subtropical fruit. We planted our first ten mango trees with no intention of ever selling anything.

Since then we've primarily focused on — and gotten a little obsessed with — mangoes. We now grow over 165 varieties, while constantly adding rare fruit and spices that choose to thrive here. We graft our own trees and hunt down the hardest-to-find varieties whenever we can. We've had plenty of failures along the way and are still learning. What started as a hobby has evolved into how we plan to retire.

In-Home Nursery & Scions

Fruit trees and scions joined what we offer in 2024, run small-scale by appointment or specific request. The 2026 freeze hit us hard and pushed us to redirect our focus toward fruiting trees, vegetables, and herbs — we're building that inventory back up to help others in the area start, or re-establish, their own edible growing journey.

Shipped scions are $6 each plus shipping, wrapped in buddy tape and labeled by variety — we can't guarantee grafting success, but we only ship fresh, viable wood. When timing allows, we also offer a grove tour with scion clipping for $3–4 per scion: we walk the trees together and you pick what you want, generally 1–2 hours by appointment depending on how much ground you'd like to cover.

Fruit, Vegetables & Cottage Foods

We only sell produce grown right here on the property. Fruit and vegetable sales are currently paused while we recover from the 2026 freeze — we'll update our catalog the moment that changes.

Our goal is to also offer jam, cookies, pastries, and dried fruit made from what we grow. That's on hold for now too, but stay tuned.
